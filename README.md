# Acheline: Headache Diary

A free, private headache journal for iPhone. Log a headache in one tap, mark where it hurts
on a 3D head, and share a clear PDF report with your doctor.

- **One-tap logging** from the app, a Home Screen widget, Control Center, Siri, or a reminder
  notification.
- **Where it hurts** on a rotatable 3D head; painful areas glow in the color of the intensity.
- **While it lasts:** Better, Same, Worse, or It's Gone, and a dark screen that's easier on
  sensitive eyes.
- **"Did it help?"** Two hours after medication, a reminder asks how your headache is.
- **My Medications** for one-tap logging of the one or two medications you usually take.
- **Doctor report** as a PDF or CSV, with patterns, medication use, and what followed each
  dose. On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, the overview can be written on device.
- **Apple Health** (optional): finished headaches are saved under Symptoms › Headache.

Everything stays on your iPhone. No account, no ads, no analytics. Free and non-commercial.

## Support

Questions, problems, or ideas? [Open an issue](https://github.com/zhorzhersi/acheline/issues/new)
and describe what happened, including your iPhone model and iOS version. Please don't post
health details you want to keep private.

## Privacy

Read the [privacy policy](PRIVACY.md). In short: Acheline collects nothing and sends
nothing. Your journal never leaves your iPhone unless you share a report yourself.

## Medical note

Acheline is a journal, not a medical device. It doesn't diagnose or recommend treatment. If a
headache is sudden and severe, or comes with weakness, confusion, or a stiff neck, seek urgent
care.
