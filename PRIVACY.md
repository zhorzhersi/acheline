# Acheline Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Acheline is a free headache journal. It is built so that your journal never leaves your
iPhone unless you choose to share it.

## What Acheline stores

Everything you record: pain levels, times, where it hurts, what it feels like, symptoms,
suspected triggers, medication, and notes. It is stored only on your iPhone, in a container
shared with Acheline's own widgets. There is no account, no server, and no cloud copy.

## What Acheline sends

Nothing. Acheline makes no network requests. It contains no analytics, advertising,
tracking, or third-party code.

## Apple Health

If you turn on **Save headaches to Health** in Settings, Acheline saves each finished
headache to the Health app as a Headache sample with its start, end, and severity. Acheline
asks only for permission to write headaches. It does not read anything from Health. Health
data is never used for advertising and never shared with anyone. You can turn this off in
Acheline's Settings or in the Health app at any time.

## Notifications

Reminders and the "Did it help?" follow-up are local notifications scheduled on your
iPhone. They are not sent through any server.

## On-device summary

On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, the overview in the doctor report can be written by
Apple's on-device model. The journal summary it reads never leaves your iPhone.

## Sharing

A PDF report or CSV file is created only when you tap Share, and only goes where you send
it. The temporary file is deleted when the share sheet closes.

## Deleting your data

Delete single entries in the app, or delete Acheline to remove the whole journal. Headaches
already saved to Health stay there until you delete them in the Health app.

## Children

Acheline doesn't collect data from anyone, including children.
